# ShelfLife

A web platform connecting local grocery stores and bakeries with students and budget-conscious residents, so surplus food near its expiry date gets sold at a discount instead of thrown away.

**SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production**

## Problem
Local stores discard edible food nearing expiry, while many people struggle to afford food.

## Solution
Vendors list near-expiry items at steep discounts. Buyers browse, claim, and pick up items, with an AI assistant to help find deals that fit their budget or diet.
